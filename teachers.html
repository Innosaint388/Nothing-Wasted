♻️ Zero Waste Food LoopTeacher guide

Learning goals (grades 5 and 6)

  • Explain why wasted food is a resource problem, not just a trash problem.
  • Rank uses of surplus food using the Food Rescue Ladder: use it up, share it, feed animals, make something, compost, trash.
  • Describe a food loop, where one step's leftovers become the next step's inputs.
  • Explain why safety checks (such as testing peanuts for mold toxin) are part of closing a loop.

Time: about 5 minutes per level, 20 minutes for all four.

Discussion questions

  1. Why is it better to take less food than to compost more food?
  2. Which items could not be shared, and why?
  3. Why did you have to throw away some peanuts even though they looked fine?
  4. What loops can you find in your school, home or community?

Content review checklist

  • Confirm each "why" and "Did you know?" statement against a source and add it to your lesson notes.
  • Check local rules for home compost, school share tables and what animals can safely eat.
  • Check that examples fit your students' region and food culture.

Answer key

No student data is collected. Progress is saved only on each device.
